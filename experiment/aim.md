### Aim of the Experiment

1. To understand the origin and coupling mechanisms of 50 Hz Power Line Interference (PLI) in biomedical instrumentation (such as ECG, EEG, and EMG acquisition systems).
2. To design and evaluate an optical / galvanic isolation barrier to eliminate common-mode ground loop circulating currents and achieve a CMRR > 120 dB in accordance with IEC 60601-1 medical electrical safety standards.
3. To design and implement real-time 50 Hz notch filters (Digital 2nd-Order IIR Biquad, Active Twin-T analog filter, and Adaptive LMS Noise Canceler) for targeted attenuation of mains hum while preserving sensitive physiological morphology (P-wave, QRS complex, T-wave).
4. To analyze and verify filter performance in a real-time 3D Virtual Reality (WebXR) biomedical laboratory workbench using dual-channel digital storage oscilloscopes (DSO) and FFT spectrum analyzers.
